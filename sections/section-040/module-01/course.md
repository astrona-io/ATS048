# Series Identity, Label Cardinality And Staleness

This module develops series Identity, Label Cardinality And Staleness. Use the explanations to connect intended behavior with responsible components and observable evidence.

## Learning Goals

- Explain how metric names and labels identify a time series.
- Distinguish missing labels, empty labels and matcher behavior.
- Relate changing labels to churn and historical continuity.
- Explain absent samples and staleness without substituting zero automatically.

## Before You Start

Examples illustrate declared relationships and reasoning. No execution environment is required or claimed.
